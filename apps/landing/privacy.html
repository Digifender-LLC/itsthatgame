Privacy notice

Draft - to be reviewed before app store submission. Version 2026-10-04.

ItsThatGame is published by Digifender LLC. This notice explains, in plain language, what we collect and why. The short version: no ads, no tracking, no selling data, and as little personal data as we can manage.

The launch waitlist (this website)

When you join the waitlist we store only:

  • your email address (and a normalized copy used to avoid duplicates);
  • the price option you chose;
  • the time you agreed to be emailed, and the version of this notice you agreed to;
  • where you came from: the utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign and utm_content values in the link you used, and the website that referred you;
  • when the entry was created and last updated.

We use this to email you about the launch and to learn which prices and channels work. This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics. A human-check widget from Cloudflare (Turnstile) runs on the sign-up and removal forms to stop bots; it is the only third-party service loaded by this site.

How long we keep it: until you ask us to remove it, or 12 months after we decide whether to build the app, whichever comes first.

Removing yourself: use the remove my email page. Removal deletes your entry.

The app

  • No accounts. You don't sign up or log in.
  • Progress and settings stay on your device. We don't back them up to any server. Uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Game requests. If you send one, we store your description, an optional link and where you saw the game, tied to a random install ID that we hash on our server. We store no name, email or IP address with requests. Network addresses used to limit abuse are hashed too.
  • Usage statistics. The app can send simple, anonymous events (for example, which game was opened) used only in aggregate or with a rotating install-scoped ID, kept at most 13 months. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google, and entitlement is checked through RevenueCat. They handle your payment details; we do not see them.

What we never do

  • Show advertising, in the app or on this site.
  • Use ad, attribution or tracking SDKs or scripts.
  • Sell or share personal data for advertising.

Children

ItsThatGame is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). If you think a child has joined the waitlist, contact us and we will delete the entry.
