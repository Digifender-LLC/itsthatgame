Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app, go to Settings, and choose "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple or Google account you bought with. Purchases are handled by the app store, so we never see your payment details.

Do the games work offline?

Yes. Once the app is installed, every game is fully playable without a connection. The only things that need the internet are sending a game request, restoring a purchase, and checking your purchase.

How do I request a game I saw in an ad?

In the app, open "Request a game" and describe the game, optionally adding a link and where you saw it. We review requests and show a status for each one. We build our own original games inspired by the idea, not copies of the original.

How do I delete my data?

Your progress and settings live only on your device; uninstalling the app removes them. To be removed from the launch waitlist, use the remove my email page. See the privacy notice for the full list of what we keep.
